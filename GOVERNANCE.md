# Governance

## Current model

runtime-upgrade is maintained by a small team. Decisions are made by the maintainers
listed below, by consensus where possible.

## How decisions get made

- **Routine changes** - one maintainer approval merges.
- **Behaviour or API changes** - open an issue first; two maintainers approve.
- **Licence, governance, or security policy changes** - all maintainers must agree.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, high-quality contribution over time, then nomination by an existing
maintainer and consensus among the rest.

## Stepping down

Open a PR removing yourself from the table above. No explanation required.
