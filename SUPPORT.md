# Support

## Questions

- Search the [issues](../../issues) first - your question may be answered.
- For usage questions, open a [discussion](../../discussions).

## Bugs

Open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose) with reproduction steps.

## Security

Do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What to expect

This is open source maintained on a best-effort basis. There is no SLA for
questions or feature requests. Security reports are prioritised - see SECURITY.md.
